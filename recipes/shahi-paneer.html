Shahi Panner

Shahi Paneer is a rich, creamy North Indian vegetarian curry from Mughlai cuisine, translating literally to "Royal Paneer" due to its luxurious ingredients and historical association with royal kitchens. It features soft cubes of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) simmered in a velvety gravy made from a base of onions, cashews, almonds, yogurt, and cream, often finished with aromatic spices like saffron, cardamom, and kewra water.

While traditional Mughlai versions use a white gravy without tomatoes, modern restaurant-style recipes frequently incorporate a tomato-based orange gravy for color and tang, similar to Paneer Butter Masala. The dish is characterized by its mild, slightly sweet flavor profile and is typically served hot with naan, roti, or jeera rice.

Ingredients

Serves

  • Paneer 500 g, cubed
  • Onions 2 medium, chopped
  • Tomatoes 3 medium, chopped
  • Cashews 15–20
  • Fresh cream ½ cup
  • Milk ½ cup
  • Butter 2 tbsp
  • Ginger-garlic paste 1 tbsp
  • Red chili powder 1 tsp
  • Garam masala ½ tsp
  • Salt to taste
  • Kasuri methi 1 tsp

Steps:

  1. Prepare the cashews: Soak the cashews in warm water for about 10–15 minutes. This makes them soft and easier to blend.

  2. Cook the onion: Heat 1 tbsp butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they become soft and lightly golden.

  3. Add ginger-garlic: Add 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste and cook for about 1 minute until the raw smell goes away.

  4. Cook tomatoes and cashews: Add the chopped tomatoes and soaked cashews. Cook for 7–8 minutes until the tomatoes become very soft.

  5. Make the gravy: Turn off the heat and let the mixture cool slightly. Blend it until you get a smooth, creamy paste.

  6. Cook the gravy: Heat the remaining 1 tbsp butter in the same pan. Add the blended paste and cook for 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  7. Add spices: Add red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Mix well and cook for another 2 minutes so the spices blend into the gravy.

  8. Add milk: Slowly add ½ cup milk while stirring. Add about ½ cup water to get the desired gravy consistency. Simmer for 5 minutes.

  9. Add paneer: Gently add the paneer cubes. Mix carefully and cook on low heat for 3–4 minutes so the paneer absorbs the gravy without becoming hard.

  10. Finish the dish: Lower the heat and add ½ cup fresh cream and 1 tsp crushed kasuri methi. Mix gently and cook for 1–2 minutes. Do not boil strongly after adding the cream.
